## The Idea Behind Skyline

I originally wrote the **Skyline algorithm** with one central purpose: to explore a way of directing resources toward people who need them most at a particular moment in their lives.

I believe Skyline works best when a community includes **people of different ages, backgrounds, and stages of life**, because our financial needs change throughout our lifetime.

A student, for example, may need additional support to study comfortably and invest time in developing their talents. Someone later in life may value additional financial security or the possibility of retiring earlier. At another moment, a family, entrepreneur, or someone experiencing financial hardship may benefit most from the resources available within the community.

Community leaders, sponsors, businesses, or other supporters could also participate—not necessarily because they personally need the resources, but because they want to **contribute to the game and help create opportunities for people who need support at that moment**.

In this way, Skyline explores a simple idea:

**What if a game could bring people with different financial means together and create a community-driven way of allocating resources where they can make the greatest difference?**
